# Yarn options for the Mandelbrot kanavatyö

Research from September 2026. The yarn has to have good colors, be reasonably affordable, and ship to Finland.
Prices are as found online and may have changed.

The piece is 120 × 90 cm at 3.9 stitches/cm, which is 468 × 351 = 164,268 half stitches. The example setup uses
12 colors and about 1,170 m of yarn for one strand, estimated by the app's yarn calculator.

## Canvas and yarn thickness

The canvas is the **Taito shop Penelope kanavakangas**: 100% cotton, 39 holes / 10 cm (3.9 st/cm), 60 or 90 cm
wide. It's a double-thread (Penelope) canvas, so each half stitch goes over one pair of threads. At this gauge that
is the classic ~10-mesh "gros point".

**The usual yarn at this gauge is about tapestry-wool thickness per stitch.** Here's what that means for each yarn:

| Yarn | Strands on 3.9/cm Penelope | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Pirkka ohut** (tex 125 × 2, 400 m/100 g) | **2 strands** | The Taito shop recommends ohut Pirkka for this canvas, and ready-made kits use it doubled |
| Pirkka paksu (tex 285 × 2, 175 m/100 g) | 1 strand | About tapestry-wool thickness; fits through Penelope holes more easily than Aida's |
| Jamieson's Spindrift (105 m/25 g) | 2 strands | About the same thickness as ohut Pirkka |
| Rauma Finull (175 m/50 g) | 2 strands (maybe 1: it's lofty) | Test both |
| Appletons Crewel | ~3 strands | Add strands until the canvas is covered |
| Persian wool (e.g. Paternayan) | 3 plies | The standard for 10-mesh gros point |

**Petit point at 7.8 st/cm** (over single threads of the same canvas, 936 × 702 stitches) needs about half that
thickness per stitch: **1 strand of ohut Pirkka, Spindrift or Finull**. There are 4× as many stitches, but each is
half as long, so the total yarn is about 2× the 3.9/cm amount for the same number of strands. The app computes
this from the stitches/cm setting.

**At 3.9/cm the default plan is 2 strands of ohut Pirkka** (or 2 of Spindrift or Finull). Set *Strands per stitch = 2* in
the app's Yarn amount settings. That doubles the yarn amounts and costs in the tables below, which are for 1
strand. Confirm with a test patch: the canvas threads shouldn't show between the stitches.

## Recommendation

**Jamieson's Spindrift (Shetland wool)** fits best:

- It has the largest color range of the affordable options, about 225 shades.
- A Finnish shop sells it, so there are no customs.
- The balls are small (105 m), so a pattern with many colors wastes little yarn.
- It's about the same thickness as Pirkka ohut.

## Caveats

- **The yarn amounts are estimates.** The app assumes one front diagonal plus one back step per stitch, times the
  strands, plus 15% for tails. Before ordering, stitch a test patch (e.g. 20 × 20) with each candidate, measure the
  yarn used, and adjust the app's Yarn amount settings.
- **Colors are approximate.** The app's colors are screen colors (Pirkka and Rauma were sampled from photos), so
  compare the real yarn before committing.
- **Customs:** UK shops (Appletons, Jamieson & Smith, Jamieson's direct) may add VAT and handling fees on import.
  Finnish and EU shops don't.
- **Stock:** individual shades go out of stock at times, so check your exact colors before buying.
